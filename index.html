The Method

AI-assisted. Not AI-created.

I use AI daily to draft, research, and move fast. But the real work happens at two points where the human input goes in, and it's never optional.

Before the script

Every idea goes back to you first. I want your real thoughts, values, and opinions, and real examples from your own life, before a single line gets written.

After the first draft

The script goes back again. This second round is where it gets refined with your actual voice, your beliefs, your stories, not a generic version of you.

AI isn't doing the whole job. It's an integral part of the writing, but the writing stays anchored in a human voice.
